## 1. Google Search Console (do this first — 10 minutes)

This is the only way to know what Google has indexed and which searches you appear in.

1. Go to https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Click **Add property** → choose **Domain** → enter `kcgaragesales.com`
3. Google shows you a TXT record (looks like `google-site-verification=abc123...`).
   Add it in your DNS settings (wherever the domain is registered — Namecheap/GoDaddy/
   Cloudflare → DNS → Add record → type TXT, host `@`, paste the value).
4. Back in Search Console, click **Verify** (DNS can take a few minutes to propagate).
5. Once verified: left menu → **Sitemaps** → enter `sitemap.xml` → Submit.
6. Left menu → **URL Inspection** → paste `https://kcgaragesales.com` → click
   **Request Indexing**. Repeat for 2–3 top city pages (e.g. `/overland-park`, `/olathe`).

Check back weekly: **Performance** tab shows the queries you appear for;
**Pages** tab shows what's indexed and why anything is excluded.

Also do the same at **Bing Webmaster Tools** (https://www.bing.com/webmasters) —
you can import the verified site straight from Search Console in 2 clicks.

Post (adjust per group rules — some ban links; if so, mention the name without a link):
